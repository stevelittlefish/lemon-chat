"Ah. So. About our heading. The autopilot is— locked. It has been locked for rather a long

had as coordinates?" Flicker, on the player's shoulder, has gone very still — its flame

half-remembered stories of the founders, Flicker pressed to the glass the whole way. Then,
ahead, a new light — and the rocket falls toward a world that is not a world of land and sea
but a world of LAMPS.
