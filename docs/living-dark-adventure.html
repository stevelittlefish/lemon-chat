Adventure overview

You wake on cold stone in a vast underground structure built by a civilisation that has been gone — or changed — for millennia. You remember nothing. A small maintenance robot called Unit 7 is watching you. It says it can help you get out.

The Vel-Ahru Complex is still alive: its lichen breathes, its circuits hum, and something drifts through the enormous caverns that isn't quite animal and isn't quite anything else. The way out requires two components and the willingness to go further in before you can come back out.

Objectives

Both item locations are open from the start. The Ascent Shaft unlocks automatically when both are found.

  1. Navigation Stone — Luminare Vaults, east. Challenge: Luminare communication encounter.
  2. Power Cell — Grub Warrens, north. Challenge: retrieve it from a sleeping Grub nest.
  3. Activate the Ascent Shaft — North-west. Auto-succeeds when both items are in hand. No random check.

Special mechanics

Memory fragments — tracked as a counter (0–3). Each successful or partial encounter adds one. The narration style shifts as the counter rises: blank and disoriented at zero, purposeful and grounded at three. Show memory status alongside health in every response.

Unit 7 trust — if the player is patient or curious with Unit 7 during the opening, unit7_trust is set to "helpful". This grants advantage on the Grub Den encounter: Unit 7 describes the safe route before the player enters. It is the only advantage source in this adventure.

Why approach tables

The Luminare encounter has no fixed difficulty. The player's described approach determines which table is used — patient and open gets the easy list, aggressive or demanding gets the hard list. This makes the encounter feel like genuine communication: the mechanics reward the behaviour that fits the fiction.

The same pattern applies here as in Eldoria's River Spirit encounter — the quality of the player's action is the input, not a dice roll.

Setup steps

  1. Go to Settings → Notes and create each of the six notes below, pasting the content exactly.
  2. After saving each note, enable Read-only for every note marked read-only.
  3. Use the existing Choose Your Own character. No new character is needed — it finds all g.cyo.* adventures automatically.
  4. Start a new conversation with Choose Your Own. It will offer The Living Dark alongside any other installed adventures.
Haven't set up Choose Your Own yet? See the Eldoria adventure guide for the full character setup and system prompt.
Easier for younger players: Before starting, edit g.cyo.living-dark and change player_health = "3" to "5". Turn read-only back on after editing.

Notes to create

Six notes total. Enter the key exactly as shown and mark read-only after saving.

g.cyo.living-dark
globalread-only
Adventure manifest — loaded first. Contains load instructions, world state, objectives, memory fragment guide, and the opening narration.
g.cyo.living-dark.rules
globalread-only
Game mechanics — randomness, health, memory fragments, Unit 7 trust, response length, tone, and image guidance.
g.cyo.living-dark.world
globalread-only
World lore — the Vel-Ahru Complex, Unit 7, all four locations, and their inhabitants.
g.cyo.living-dark.bestiary
globalread-only
Encounter cards — load when the player first approaches a creature or attempts an encounter. Defines approach tables and outcomes for both challenges.
g.cyo.living-dark.secrets
globalread-onlyGM only
GM-only lore — never revealed directly. Informs the tone of the complex, Unit 7's manner, and the Luminares' behaviour.
g.cyo.living-dark.victory
globalread-only
Victory narration — read aloud when the Ascent Shaft carries the player to the surface.

Starting play

  1. Start a new conversation with Choose Your Own.
  2. It will present available adventures. Say "The Living Dark".
  3. The GM loads lore, sets world state, generates an opening image, and reads the intro. Play begins.
  4. Each response ends with three choices. Pick one, or type something else.
If Unit 7 seems cold or unhelpful: that is its default. Ask it questions — about the complex, about what it has observed, about the Luminares. It has been waiting a long time and has a great deal to say, in its way. Patience with it is mechanically rewarded.
If the GM declares an outcome before rolling: say "You need to record the outcomes before calling pick_random — check the rules note."

Adjusting difficulty

Turn off read-only, edit, then re-enable read-only.

  • Easier: In g.cyo.living-dark, change player_health = "3" to "5".
  • Easier: In g.cyo.living-dark.bestiary, change the Grub Den list to EASY.
  • Harder: Change starting health to "2".
  • Harder: Remove the healing herb entry from g.cyo.living-dark.rules.
  • Much harder: In g.cyo.living-dark.world, remove the detail that the Grub Warrens is NORTH. The player must discover the layout themselves.
