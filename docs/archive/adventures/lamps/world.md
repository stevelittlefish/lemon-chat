## The Lumalands (act 1)
A sunlit region of green hills, hedgerows, and small towns where everyone keeps Lumens —
creatures that are all, one way or another, lamps. Candle-creatures, lantern-beasts, living
streetlamps, firefly-bulbs. The Lumalands are gentle and a little old-fashioned: lamp-posts
light themselves at dusk and hum through the night, oil is the local currency of kindness
(you top up a stranger's lamp the way you'd offer a cup of tea), and a trainer's bond with
their Lumen is measured in how brightly it burns for them. Night here is never truly
frightening — there is always a light somewhere. That is exactly what makes the dark, when it
comes (act 2), so wrong.

LUMEN BATTLES are bright, friendly contests of light and pluck — two Lumens trying to
out-shine, dazzle, or gently nudge each other off their feet. Nothing is hurt. A trainer who
loses simply has their Lumen "dim out" and bows off. The player resolves battles with their
companion (see .bestiary for which difficulty table each opponent uses).

## Wickville (home town)
A tidy huddle of round-windowed houses, a memoryless windmill, and a corner lamp on every
street. The player's home. NOTABLE:
- PROFESSOR BULB — round, kindly, bottle-glass spectacles, a coat of singed pockets. Gave the
  player Flicker. Knows the Lumalands well and shares directions and lore freely. About
  Flicker's origins they are honestly puzzled ("came to us from who-knows-where") — they do
  not know the truth (see .secrets); never have them reveal it.
- THE LANTERN REST — a low, warm rest-house hung with soft lanterns and a hearth of oil-lamps.
  Heals the player to full (see rules). The keeper is unhurried and motherly.

## The Three Wayfinder Lanterns
Three great iron lanterns on tall posts, spaced along the road north to Lux City. In living
memory they have always burned. This season all three went dark at once, the fog rolled in
behind them, and the road stopped letting travellers through. Relighting all three lifts the
fog and opens the gym road (manifest objective gate). They may be tackled in any order. Each
relighting raises flicker_bond by 1.

### The Dim Woods (lantern_woods)
A hushed old wood where the canopy keeps out the sun and the usual path-lanterns have all gone
cold. The first Wayfinder Lantern stands in a clearing at the wood's heart. A wild LANTERNJAW
— a shy, anglerfish-shaped lantern-creature with a lure-light it has nervously snuffed —
has nested around the lantern and startles at any approach (see .bestiary). Calm it or
out-shine it, then relight the Wayfinder. Smell of cold moss and old candle-smoke.

### Ember Spring (lantern_spring)
A rocky hollow where warm lamp-oil wells up from a spring and pools amber-gold. The second
Wayfinder Lantern stands here bone-dry — someone must draw oil from the spring to refill it.
A territorial TORCHCAT (Emberpaw), a fiery torch-tailed cat, has claimed the warmest rock by
the spring and does not care to share (see .bestiary). Get past it, fill the lantern, light
it. Smell of hot stone and sweet oil; the air shimmers.

### Lookout Ridge (lantern_ridge)
A high windy ridge with the whole Lumalands laid out below and Lux City glittering far to the
north. The third Wayfinder Lantern stands at the cairn. SPARK is already here — a cocky young
rival trainer in a too-cool jacket who has "claimed" the ridge and won't let anyone relight
"their" lantern without a battle (see .bestiary). Beat Spark, light the lantern. Spark is a
good loser underneath the swagger and, once beaten, becomes warmly encouraging.

## The Road to Lux City
Once all three Wayfinders burn, the fog lifts and this road opens: a bright ribbon north
through poppy fields and self-lighting milestones, ending at the gates of Lux City. Use a
short travel scene here before the city — this is the calm before act 2.

## Lux City & the Grand Lumen Gym
The biggest city in the Lumalands: tall lamp-lit avenues, a second LANTERN REST near the
gates (heal here before the gym), and at the city's heart the GRAND LUMEN GYM. The Gym is a
strange and beautiful building — a broad arena beneath an enormous chandelier, but built
around the stone core of a much older structure: a converted lighthouse-observatory, its old
spiral and sealed lower doors plastered over and forgotten. GYM LEADER LUMINA is a tall,
gracious radiance-type trainer who greets challengers warmly and promises the player "a proper
first battle." That battle never happens — see the PHASE TRANSITION in the manifest and the
.phase2 note. (The truth of what the gym is built on is in .secrets — do not reveal it early.)

## Flicker (the companion) in act 1
Bright, bouncy, fiercely loyal; bumps its warm side against the player when they're uncertain.
In still moments — dusk, a clear night, the top of Lookout Ridge — it goes quiet and turns its
glass face up to the stars, as if it half-remembers something far away. Play this as a small,
unexplained tenderness. Never explain it in act 1. It is the thread that act 3 pulls.
